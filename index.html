What is this?

    
A workshop tool for publishing rich HTML bios and script pages to janitorai.com through its API — the method documented by DelightfulTempe (see their Unrestricted Bot Bio Guide) and inspired by Dezea's JAI_Publisher. The FORGE runs entirely in your browser:

    
    
      
  1. Preview runs on a local snapshot of JAI's stylesheet — nothing is uploaded while you edit.
    2. 
      
  2. Token and HTML live in your browser's local storage only.
    3. 
      
  3. Publishing generates a first-party console script — your token goes from your browser to janitorai.com and nowhere else. No relay, no third party.
    4. 
    

    
Be decent: only publish to characters and scripts you own, keep layouts in-container, and don't break the page for visitors. The method survives by not being abused.

    
Lint checks for server-stripped constructs (position, z-index, <style>, <script>) and layout manners before you publish.
